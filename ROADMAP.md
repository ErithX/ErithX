# ErithX Roadmap

ErithX started as a contest tracker and now gives weekly reviews of your coding activity. The next stage adds a public profile, community, deeper AI, and a resume-free path to being hired.

Status values: **Planned**, **In progress**, **Released**.

---

## Priority overview

| Part | Focus | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Profile and personalized contests | P0 | Planned |
| 2 | Spaces and leaderboard | P1 | Planned |
| 3 | AI Space and GitHub project guidance | P1 | Planned |
| 6 | Pro and Ultra plans | P1 | Planned |
| 4 | Opportunity discovery | P2 | Planned |
| 5 | Resume-free hiring | P2 | Planned |

---

## Part 1: Your identity (P0)

### Profile page
- Public profile with your details and stats across platforms
- Followers and following
- Shareable metric cards for LinkedIn, X, resume, and portfolio

### Personalized contests
- Star a contest to get an email alert before it starts
- Pre-contest analysis: what to revise, what to expect, how to prepare
- Takeaways that apply beyond contests: time management, focus under pressure, consistency

---

## Part 2: Community (P1)

### Spaces and leaderboard
- Spaces to view other users' progress and profiles
- Leaderboards across friends, colleges, and the full community
- Follow users and track how they practice

---

## Part 3: AI and project growth (P1)

### AI Space
- Replaces static cards with an AI that works from your profile
- Finds contests that fit you, researches them, and analyzes them against your history
- Answers questions in plain language using your own data

### Project guidance and GitHub integration
- Connect GitHub to review code quality and documentation
- Generate resume bullets from your real work
- Tasks that push you to add measurable results (latency, scale, users)
- Project progress feeds your hiring profile (see Part 5)

---

## Part 4: Opportunity discovery (P2)

### Hidden challenges, hackathons, and global contests
- Deeper coverage of company hiring challenges that are hard to find
- Hackathons and contests from around the world
- Matched to your profile, with alerts

---

## Part 5: Resume-free hiring (P2, long-term)

### Focus score and hidden stats
- A score built from consistency and progress over time, not keywords
- Stats hidden from the public, visible to hiring partners

### Hiring platform
- Companies and hiring managers can search top candidates directly
- No resume screening and no keyword filters
- Ranking based on consistency, progress, project quality, and code health
- Candidates control their visibility

---

## Part 6: Plans (P1, launches with Part 3)

| Plan | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Contest tracking and weekly review |
| **Pro** | Advanced AI, GitHub project analysis, contest post-mortems, deeper personalization |
| **Ultra** | Everything in Pro, plus hiring and discovery features |

**Early user reward:** every existing user gets all advanced features free for a set period after launch. The duration will be announced at launch.
