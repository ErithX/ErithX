### 4. My Recommendations on Your Questions

**Template Aesthetics:**  
→ **Option A (Classic Minimalist – White background)**  
Dark mode emails frequently break across Gmail, Outlook, and mobile clients. White/light is safer and more professional for deliverability.

**Newsletter & Product Updates:**  
→ **Option A (Manual via Resend Dashboard)** for now.  
You’re still early. No need to build an admin broadcasting system yet.

**AI Mentor Alert Tone:**  
→ Mix of both, leaning toward clear + human.  
Not pure robotic (“Your review is ready”), and not overly casual.  
Best style:  
“Your weekly review is ready. Here’s the honest summary.”
