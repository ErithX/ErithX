# Security Policy

If you discover a security vulnerability, please disclose it privately:

- Email: founder@erithx.dev (preferred) or debjyoti2409@gmail.com
- PGP/GPG: (optional) provide a key on request for encrypted reports.

Report contents:
- A short summary of the issue
- Steps to reproduce with minimal proof-of-concept
- Impact and suggested mitigation
- Affected versions/commit SHA

Our response policy:
- Acknowledgement within 48 hours
- Initial triage within 5 business days
- Fix or mitigation plan within 30 days (or earlier for critical vulnerabilities)
- We will credit reporters who follow responsible disclosure unless they request anonymity.

Do not publish exploits publicly until we have had a reasonable time to fix.
